Partnership Request Portal

Work with us to improve literacy, strengthen education, and create better learning opportunities for all.

Submit a Partnership Request

Schools, NGOs, Community based organizations, International organizations, Government Ministries and agencies, Corporate entities, and private individuals can use this portal to request a partnership with Smart Readers Liberia, Inc. All requests are subject to review and approval.

How It Works

  1. Fill out your partnership request form.
  2. Upload the required supporting documents.
  3. Submit your request for review.
  4. SRL reviews your application.
  5. The Technical Review Team makes the final decision.

Before You Apply

  • Provide accurate organization details.
  • Briefly explain the purpose of the partnership.
  • Describe the expected benefits.
  • Attach your supporting documents.
  • Provide a working email address and phone number.

Partnership Application Form

Complete all required fields before submitting.

Upload a summary of your organizational profile, business registration document, partnership proposal, or other relevant files. PDF and Word documents are accepted. Keep the total file size below 4 MB.

Partnership Review and Approval

Every request must pass through the SRL review process. Only the technical review team can grant final approval. Submission does not guarantee approval.
