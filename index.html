Welcome to Smart Readers Liberia, Inc.

Welcome to our home online.

We are glad you are here.

Smart Readers Liberia, Inc. (SRL) is a Liberian nonprofit. We were founded in February 2021. Our headquarters is on MC Caro Street. This is in the New Israel Community, Johnsonville Township, Montserrado County.

We believe literacy is a fundamental right. This belief guides everything we do.

Our Mission and Vision

Our Programs.

We run nine evidence-based programs. They serve children, youth, adults, and teachers across Greater Monrovia and beyond.

We are a team of passionate educators, volunteers, and community members. We are committed to making a difference in Liberia. We believe that literacy is the key to unlocking potential and creating a brighter future for all.

We invite you to explore our website. Learn about our programs, meet our team, and see how you can get involved. Together, we can create a more literate and empowered Liberia.

We welcome partnerships. NGOs, UN agencies, and government institutions are all welcome. Donors, schools, and community organizations are welcome too.

Together, we can bring literacy to every Liberian.
