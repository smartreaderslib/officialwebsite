MESSAGE FROM THE FOUNDER

Welcome to our home online. We are glad you are here!

At Smart Readers Liberia, Inc., we believe that every learner deserves the opportunity to read, write, learn, and succeed. Education is more than the ability to read words or pass examinations. It is the foundation for confidence, independence, opportunity, and lifelong success.

Smart Readers Liberia, Inc. was founded on February 9, 2021, with a simple but powerful belief that every learner can succeed when given the right support, guidance, and opportunity. What began as a commitment to improving literacy has grown into a broader mission to promote inclusive education and lifelong learning.

On June 23, 2023, we officially launched our organization at Versenia Mission School in Johnsonville, Liberia. This milestone marked an important step in our journey to support struggling readers, strengthen literacy instruction, and expand educational opportunities for learners in our communities.

Since our founding, we have remained committed to helping learners overcome literacy challenges and develop the knowledge and skills they need to build a better future. We understand that every learner is different. Some need extra time to learn to read. Others need support with writing, digital skills, or academic development. We are committed to creating inclusive learning opportunities that recognize these differences and help every learner reach their potential.

Our work goes beyond the classroom. Through literacy development, teacher training, digital education, environmental awareness, financial literacy, and practical skills development, we seek to empower individuals and strengthen communities across Liberia.

However, we cannot achieve this mission alone. We need the support of parents, teachers, school administrators, volunteers, donors, development partners, and everyone who believes in the power of education. Together, we can create more opportunities for struggling readers, support teachers, and build a future where no learner is left behind.

I invite you to join us in this important mission. Whether you partner with us, volunteer your time, support our programs, or help spread our message, your contribution can make a meaningful difference in someone's life.

Our founding on February 9, 2021, and official launch on June 23, 2023, remind us of the commitment that started this journey and the responsibility we carry to continue growing our impact. We remain determined to reach more learners, strengthen partnerships, and make quality, inclusive education accessible to more communities.

At Smart Readers Liberia, Inc., every learner matters, every opportunity counts, and every step toward literacy brings us closer to a brighter future.

Thank you for believing in our vision and joining us on this journey toward a more literate, inclusive, and empowered Liberia. I encourage you to take up a little of your time to explore our online home and learn more about where we came from, where we are now, and where we intend to be in the next 10 years.

Our commitment remains clear: Inclusive Education for Lifelong Success.

With purpose and dedication,


Nelson T. Sackie
Founder & Executive Director
Smart Readers Liberia, Inc.
